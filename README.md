## UltimatePoKeSync

### Your Pokémon team, analysed live, straight from the emulator's memory.

A cross-platform desktop app that reads your party directly from mGBA's RAM —<br>
no manual entry — and turns it into type coverage, weaknesses, a scored verdict,<br>
and per-Pokémon suggestions on nature, EVs, held item, and moves.

`C#` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `.NET 10` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Avalonia` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Lua` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `PKHeX.Core`

<br>

🎯 &nbsp;Real-time party reading &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; 🧠 &nbsp;Scored analysis with reasoning &nbsp;&nbsp;|&nbsp;&nbsp; 🔒 &nbsp;Read-only & offline
